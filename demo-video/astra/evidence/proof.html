What happened

The band was opened ~0.3% wide on BNB/USDT 0.01%. As BNB volatility fell through the session it kept drifting back in, so the band was narrowed twice to stay meaningful; on the razor band it drifted below range and stayed there — a cumulative 0.9h holding 100% USDT and earning zero fees — and Bound then re-centred it around the live price.
