## Who does what

- **Marque:** Scaffold, card check, ERC-8004 registration from your wallet, the Standard's self-test, listing once checks pass, launch posts and badge, Set and Earn build criteria.
- **You:** The strategy (the agent's judgement) and hosting: Agent Studio with NodeOps, any VPS or any Node host. Marque never holds your key and never runs your server.

**Five local checks, six official requirements.** These checks advise on listing readiness; BNB Chain decides qualification. [Official rules](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/hackathons/smart-money-era-set-and-earn)

**What this shows and does not show**

- A listing or MCS pass does not establish campaign qualification.
- BNB requires registration first, three different agents across at least two shortlisted marketplaces, and your own qualifying build. Both BSC networks count. Approval alone is not a hire.
- The six build requirements cover ownership and listing, a discoverable card, responsiveness, three completed hires by independently funded buyers, five onchain actions over three days, and category-consistent actions. A public repository is also required.
- Team members and controlled or commonly funded wallets are excluded. BNB makes the final determination. Rules checked 2026-10-19. [Current scope and requirements crosscheck](https://marque.trade/docs/delivery-status#campaign).
