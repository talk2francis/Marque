Fifty-four HTTP requests comprised 52 successes and two redirects, with zero completed HTTP errors and no persistent restart changes. No higher stage ran. The aborted 2.46 requests/second is not sustained throughput. The actual-stage observer captured one catalogue query leader, versus three in the earlier failure; removing duplicate catalogue refreshes did not resolve latency.

A separate capped compiled worker used read-only databases and its own Redis. Its concurrent diagnostic requested Job Rooms outside the benchmark set, plus Judge, an agent profile and search. The trace associated more than 100 RPC calls with work initiated under the Judge request. The proof projection visits delivered jobs, and expired ten-minute Lens entries can re-read original receipts and answers even though complete saved checks already describe their original block. The CPU sample also contains catalogue decoding, garbage collection and startup warm reads. Capping, profiler overhead and overlapping startup activity prevent treating its timings as capacity or assigning every delay to one source.

The candidate reuses only complete saved Lens results with matching manifests, consistent check counts and a recorded block for chain checks. A supplied current commitment must match. Original dates, failed checks and cache retention stay intact. Missing, unsaved, unverified, incomplete and unpinned chain results keep their ordinary refresh. Explicit replay and current-chain comparisons bypass this reuse, and live quotes, balances, allowances, wallet ownership and transaction simulation are unchanged. Redis TTL is not extended and old evidence is not re-dated as current.

Ladder, incomplete Lens and live-example stale refreshes also use the existing ownership-fenced cross-process lease. Their freshness intervals and request deadlines remain unchanged. Live-example generation still searches the same borrowers and positions and returns the same up-to-five lists. Its existing 25-second request budget now reaches the actual RPC transport and is checked between scan steps, so expired work cannot keep walking more candidates. Cancellation rejects the projection rather than publishing partial or empty replacement data. Ordinary shared RPC clients keep their existing transport, endpoint timeouts and failover behavior; a cancelled scoped read does not bench an otherwise healthy endpoint.

Local tests cover stored negative evidence, original dates and retention, incomplete/live refreshes, current-commitment mismatches, endpoint timeout plus scan cancellation, ordinary transport reads, and preservation of the five-example result. Search/count/standing/quote-expiry regressions remain required. The existing 48-file source fingerprint is regenerated to match the imported chain/database source; no grading arithmetic, tolerance or issued historical bundle changes. This is a candidate, not a deployed fix or capacity pass. Exact-source CI, compiled acceptance, normal deployment and a guarded external retest remain separate gates.

The timeout/refresh audit must continue for other expensive projections. The retained trace alone does not establish that every background task is cancelled or that all remaining latency has been removed. No benchmark job is selectively warmed, and no workload, admission setting or latency target is relaxed.
